Plant Specifications & Care Instructions

Botanical Name: Aglaonema commutatum. Native to the tropical and subtropical regions of Asia and New Guinea. Known commonly as Chinese evergreens, they have been grown as luck-bringing ornamental plants in Asia for centuries.

Light Requirements

Thrives in low to bright indirect light. Avoid intense direct sunlight which can scorch variegated foliage.

Watering Schedule

Water when the top 50% of the soil feels dry to the touch. Drought tolerant, better to underwater than overwater.

Toxicity & Pet Care

Contains calcium oxalate crystals. Keep out of reach of curious cats and dogs.
